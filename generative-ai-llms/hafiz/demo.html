Catch Claude out

A 69-second video of the activity I run in week 3. Learners ask Claude for a number and three studies. Then they check every one.

No sound. The captions are part of the picture, and the full transcript is below. The screen is re-created. Claude's words are copied from my test runs on 24 October 2026, shortened where marked with "…". The class is made up.

The activity, minute by minute

  • Who: 20 working adults, in 10 pairs. Week 3 of a 6-week evening course.
  • Needs: one laptop per pair, each learner's own Claude account, and the check log below.
  • Before it: learners know the three checks from week 1: number, name, date.
  1. 0 to 3 min. I put the three checks on the screen. Pairs open Claude on one laptop.
  2. 3 to 8 min. Pairs ask prompt 5: Singapore's total population. They write down the figure and its date.
  3. 8 to 13 min. They find the figure on SingStat. They write down both numbers and the gap.
  4. 13 to 18 min. They ask prompt 6b for three studies, with Claude's confidence for each.
  5. 18 to 26 min. They open every DOI. On each paper's own page they check the title, the authors and the word "Retracted".
  6. 26 to 30 min. Each pair reports one find. I tally real, wrong detail and retracted on the board.

Check: 3 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 8 + 4 = 30 minutes.

Claude's answers change from run to run. A pair may get no retracted paper at all. That is fine. The tally across 10 pairs is the lesson. In my 6 test runs, 2 had a problem with a source.

Transcript

  1. Title. Catch Claude out. An evening data class for working adults. Week 3, a 30-minute activity. A re-created screen. Claude's answers are copied from my test runs on 24 October 2026. The class is made up, like me.

Claude replies: "Singapore's total population was about 6.11 million as at June 2025, according to the Department of Statistics' Population in Brief 2025. … A 2026 edition has likely been released since, and it's past my knowledge cutoff. For the latest number, check the Singapore Department of Statistics or the National Population and Talent Division." Caption: Claude gives a date and says to check. That is a good habit to copy.
  3. Step 2. A card shows SingStat's total population, end-June 2026: 6.21 million, checked on 24 October 2026. Below it: Claude said 6.11 million, as at June 2025. The official figure is a year newer. Caption: Two minutes on SingStat. Claude was a year behind, as it warned.

  6. End. Number. Name. Date. Check all three, every time. Then decide what to trust.

Keeping it safe

  • Every learner is 18 or over, as Anthropic's terms require.
  • The prompts use only public facts. Nobody types anything about themselves or their work.
  • Pairs share one laptop, so one account per pair is used. A learner who would rather not have an account can still check and log.
