Otter Dive: teacher guide

Pupils make a cardboard box with a crank. One cam inside lifts a creature slowly, then drops it. For Sec 1, 32 pupils in 16 pairs, in one 60-minute lesson. Any room with tables works. No code, no power.

Materials, per pair

  • 1 frame strip, 375 × 90 mm box card, pre-cut and pre-scored
  • 1 template on A4 card, printed at 100%
  • 2 bamboo skewers, tips snipped flat
  • 1 flat wooden coffee stirrer, 1 drinking straw
  • Masking tape, 1 ruler, 2 pairs of scissors, markers

Shared by the class: 2 low-temperature glue guns on silicone mats, at one station. Also PVA, and a ball of Blu Tack per table to poke holes into.

Before the lesson, about 45 minutes

  • Cut and score 16 frame strips with a craft knife on a cutting mat. Cut away from your hand.
  • Cut the slot in each top panel to fit your stirrers.
  • Snip the points off 32 skewers with side cutters.
  • Build one demo. Turn it in front of the class.

Safety

  • Pupils use scissors only. All knife work is done before class.
  • Skewers stay below chin height. No sword fights: a skewer waved is a skewer taken.
  • Hot glue only at the station, one pupil per gun, nozzle on the mat. Burns: cool running water for 20 minutes, then tell me.
  • Poke holes with a pencil into Blu Tack, never towards a hand.

The 60 minutes

0 to 5Hook. I turn the demo. "What made it drop so fast?" Pairs guess.
5 to 10Safety rules and roles: one cutter, one builder. Swap at minute 30.
10 to 20Fold the tube, tape the tab. Poke axle holes. Add straw bearings.
20 to 30Pick a creature and a cam to match its story. Cut 2 cam layers, glue, thread the skewer.
30 to 40Foot on the stirrer. Slide it through the slot. Fix the creature and wave.
40 to 48Test: 3 full turns. Measure travel with a ruler. Fix one problem.
48 to 55Swap boxes. Turn another pair's build and leave one sticky note.
55 to 60Prompts 1 and 2 on the sheet. Skewers in the cup, glue guns off and unplugged.

Pitfalls and fixes

  • Cam slips on the axle. Two layers of card, a tape flag each side.
  • Axle wobbles. Hole too big. Add a card washer, or move the hole.
  • Creature spins. Use the flat stirrer, not a skewer.
  • Snail cam jams. It turns one way only. Draw a "this way" arrow.
  • Foot catches on the cam. The cam rides off a small foot. Use the 30 mm disc.
  • Box squashes. Tape the tab along its whole length.
  • Travel is less than the template says. Card squashes. Ask why, then move on.

To make it easier, give out cams already cut. To make it harder, put two cams on one axle, half a turn apart.
