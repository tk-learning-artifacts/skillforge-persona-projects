My automaton: what I made and what I learned

Name: ________________   Partner: ________________   Class: ______

1. Draw your cam. Draw an arrow for the way it turns. Label where the creature is highest.

2. How far did your creature travel in one turn? Write the number. Was it more or less than the template said? Why do you think so?

3. What story does your automaton tell? Why did you pick that cam for it?

4. What broke or went wrong? What did you change to fix it?

5. Another pair turned your box. What did they notice or get confused by?

6. If you added a second cam, what would the second movement be? Sketch it.
