Cutting template

Print this on A4 card at 100%. Turn off "fit to page". Then measure the scale line. It should be 50 mm. If it isn't, the cams will be the wrong size and the travel won't match.

The cams match the simulator exactly. Each cross marks the axle hole. The arrow on the snail cam shows the only way it can turn.
