Card template

Print this on A4 card, landscape, at 100%. This is the inside of the card. Fold it in half along the middle line, with the print inside. Pupils draw their own front cover.

The right page holds the circuit. The dashed lines show where the copper tape goes. The dots show where each LED leg sits: the long leg on the + line. The green line marks the corner flap that works as the switch.

Some printers leave a blank edge. If the tip of the + line is cut off, pupils carry the tape on to the corner by hand. The AI note is left off the printout so the card has room. It is in the corner of the left page instead.
