Lantern Lights: teacher guide

Each pupil makes a thank-you card with two paper lanterns that glow when the corner is pressed. It is a parallel circuit of copper tape, two LEDs and one coin cell. For P5, a class of 36, in a 60-minute double period. No code, no soldering, no heat.

Materials for 36 pupils

  • 36 templates printed on A4 card (plus 6 spare)
  • About 20 m of 5 mm copper tape, about 55 cm per pupil
  • 80 red LEDs, 5 mm (2 each, plus spares)
  • 40 CR2032 coin cells, 3 V, in a numbered egg tray
  • Clear tape, round-tip scissors, pencils
  • Scrap card strips for the corner-fold practice

Before the lesson

  • Print the templates. Fold one card and build it yourself.
  • Number the egg tray cups 1 to 40 and load the cells.
  • Cut tape into lengths: 25 cm for +, 20 cm for −, 10 cm for practice, per pupil.
  • Ask the form teacher which staff the cards will go to.

Safety

  • Cells come out at step 4 only. Tick each number out and back in. Count all of them before anyone leaves.
  • A swallowed button battery can burn through a child's food pipe in 2 hours. No cells in mouths or pockets. A missing cell stops the class until it is found.
  • Cards stay in school and are given to adults. No loose cells go home.
  • Round-tip scissors only. LED legs are sharp: bend them flat, never poke.

The 60 minutes

0 to 5Settle. I press my card. "What has to be true for this to light?"
5 to 10Cell rules and the help ladder. Pass round one cell to look at.
10 to 15I fold a corner under the visualiser. Everyone folds one on scrap.
15 to 20Step 1: trace the paths, mark + and −. I walk one lap and check.
20 to 32Steps 2 and 3: lay both tracks, one piece each.
32 to 42Steps 4 and 5: cell out from the tray, then the LEDs, long leg on +.
42 to 50Step 6: fold and press. Dark cards climb the help ladder.
50 to 55Prompts 1 to 3 on the reflection sheet.
55 to 60Count the cells. Cards in the class box. Tape scraps in the bin.

Pitfalls and fixes

  • Tape cut at a corner. The join is weak. Fold corners, or lay one piece over the join.
  • LED backwards. It won't light but isn't harmed. Turn it round.
  • Tracks touch. A short circuit. Fit a pencil between them all the way.
  • Cell upside down. + side up, towards the flap.
  • Flap tape stops at the fold. Carry it on to the tip.
  • Leg not taped. It flickers. Tape every leg flat.
  • LEDs in a row, not side by side. Two need more than 3 V. Keep them in parallel.

The help ladder

What did you expect? Trace the path from the + side. Where does your finger stop? Ask your partner to trace it too.
