Spot the signs of a scam

Scammers send messages that look real. Most of them use the same few tricks. Learn the 4 signs, then test yourself in the quiz.

The 4 signs

  • Hurry!

    "Pay within 2 hours." "Act now." A rush stops you from thinking or asking someone.

  • Asks for secrets

    A password, a login code (OTP) or bank details. Never send them to anyone who asks by message.

  • Too good to be true

    A prize you never entered. $300 a day for liking videos. If it sounds too good, it probably is a scam.

  • Strange link

    A link with odd spelling, or one you did not ask for. Don't tap it. Go to the real app or website yourself.

Remember ACT

ACT is Singapore's national anti-scam reminder. It started in January 2023.

  • Add

    Add protection

    Ask your parents about the ScamShield app. It screens out scam calls and flags suspicious texts.

  • Check

    Check first

    Look for the 4 signs. Check with the real company through its own app or website, not the link you were sent.

  • Tell

    Tell someone

    Tell a parent or teacher. Warn your family and friends about the scam.

Not only older people

51,501 scam cases were reported in Singapore in 2024.

70.9% of scam victims that year were under 50. That includes young people.

Source: Singapore Police Force, Annual Scams and Cybercrime Brief 2024.
