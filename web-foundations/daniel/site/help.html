Not sure? Get help

You don't have to work it out alone. Stop, then check with someone.

If a message worries you

  1. Stop

    Don't tap the link, pay, or send any code or password. Hang up if it is a call.

  2. Ask a trusted adult

    Show a parent, a teacher or another adult you trust. Show them the whole message.

  3. Check with ScamShield

    Call the ScamShield Helpline. It is open 24 hours a day, for questions about scams.

  4. Tell and report

    Report the fake account to the app it came from. Warn your friends.

ScamShield Helpline

1799

From overseas, call +65 6869 1799. You can also read about scams on the ScamShield website.

Already paid or shared something?

Don't hide it. Scammers want you to feel ashamed so you stay quiet.

  • Tell a parent right away.
  • If bank details were shared, call the bank at once. The 1799 helpline can put you straight through to seven banks.
  • A police report is made online, on the Singapore Police Force's e-services. The helpline cannot make one for you.
