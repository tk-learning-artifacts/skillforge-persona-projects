Agent rules

  1. Use an agent name. Keep your real name off your page.
  2. Share codes, never passwords.
  3. Stuck for 2 minutes? Ask your partner, then your trainer.
  4. Done early? Help a stuck agent, then try a Mastermind challenge.

True story

Julius Caesar sent secret letters over 2,000 years ago. A Roman writer called Suetonius says how. Caesar swapped each letter for the one three places later. A became D, and B became E.
