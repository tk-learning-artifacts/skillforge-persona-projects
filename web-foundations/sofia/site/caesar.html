    Crack the notes

    Our trainers left three notes. Paste each one into the box, choose Reveal, and turn the wheel until it makes sense.

    • Rookie Shift 3, like Caesar.
      PHHW DW WKH VQDFN WDEOH
      Show the answer

      MEET AT THE SNACK TABLE

    • Agent A tiny shift.
      MPPL VOEFS UIF SFE DIBJS
      Show the answer

      LOOK UNDER THE RED CHAIR. The shift is 1.

    • Mastermind No clue at all.
      LBH NER N GEHR PBQR OERNXRE
      Show the answer

      YOU ARE A TRUE CODE BREAKER. The shift is 13. Hide it with 13 again and you get the note back. Why?
