Morse lab

Morse code turns each letter into short and long signals. A short one is a dot. A long one is a dash. You can send them with a lamp, a buzzer or a tap on the table.

    The timing rules

    • A dash lasts as long as 3 dots.
    • Leave 1 dot of quiet between signals in a letter.
    • Leave 3 dots of quiet between letters.
    • Leave 7 dots of quiet between words.

    The lamp above follows these rules. One dot lasts a quarter of a second.

    The Morse chart

      Table tap game

      1. Sit back to back with your partner.
      2. Tap your agent name on the table. A tap is a dot. A knock with your fist is a dash.
      3. Your partner writes the dots and dashes, then uses the chart to read your name.
      4. Swap. Who can send a 5-letter word with no mistakes?

      True story

      On 24 May 1844, Samuel Morse sent a message by electric telegraph. It went from Washington to Baltimore in the United States. It said "What hath God wrought?" The paper tape is in the Library of Congress.
