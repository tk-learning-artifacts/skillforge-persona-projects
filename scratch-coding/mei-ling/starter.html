Why these 3 tasks

My club meets for 90 minutes. Pairs need 10 to 15 minutes for each task. The tasks go from snapping to building:

  • Task 1 is a loop. The blocks are given. Pupils only put them in order.
  • Task 2 is an if block and a variable. Pupils find the blocks, with a picture to copy.
  • Task 3 is a countdown and a broadcast. It puts loops, variables and events together.
  • The stretch adds the recycling rule. That needs if-else and costume numbers.

Blue Bin Catch: task card

Work in pairs. Swap the iPad or mouse at each whistle. Press the green flag, then Start, to test after every task.

Task 1: Make the rubbish fall

Click the Item sprite. Find the 3 loose blocks. Snap them under "show" in the clone script, like this:

show
repeat until y position < -170
change y by -4
delete this clone

Hint: "repeat until" keeps going until the item reaches the bottom. Then the clone deletes itself.

Check: rubbish falls and disappears at the bottom.

Task 2: Catch it and score

Inside "repeat until", under "change y by -4", add an if block:

change y by -4
if touching Bin ? then
change score by 1
start sound pop
delete this clone

Hint: "touching" is in Sensing (light blue). Pick Bin from its menu. "change score by 1" is in Variables (orange).

Check: catch something and the score goes up 1.

Task 3: Stop after 30 seconds

Click the Stage. Add a countdown under "set time left to 30":

set time left to 30
repeat until time left = 0
wait 1 seconds
change time left by -1
broadcast game over
switch backdrop to end

Hint: the "=" block is in Operators (green). Drop the orange "time left" block into its left side. Type 0 on the right.

Check: time left stops at 0, and all rubbish vanishes.

Stretch: Only recyclables score

Right now a tissue scores a point too. Costumes 1 to 4 can be recycled. Costumes 5 to 7 can't. Change your if block so the wrong items take a point away:

if touching Bin ? then
if costume number < 5 then
change score by 1
start sound pop
else
change score by -1
start sound buzz
delete this clone

Hint: "costume number" is in Looks (purple), at the bottom.

Still faster? Make the rubbish fall faster when time left is under 10.

I tested the card on a colleague

The colleague and the pupils here are made up, like me.

A science teacher who had never used Scratch tried the card on an iPad. I watched and said nothing.

  • Task 1 took her 4 minutes. She put "delete this clone" inside the loop first. The rubbish vanished straight away. She moved it out on her own.
  • Task 2 took 9 minutes. She could not find "touching". The hint said "Sensing" but not the colour. I added "(light blue)" to every hint.
  • Task 3 took 12 minutes. She typed "time left" into the "=" block as words. Then it never stopped. I added the line "Drop the orange time left block".
  • She did not try the stretch. She said the costume numbers were "a bit magic". So the stretch now says which numbers are which.

The first version of the card also had a fourth task, a high score. Nobody got near it. I cut it.
