The change notes

I write down every change, so anyone can check it or undo it.

  1. Kept the raw file. I downloaded it from data.gov.sg on 24 October 2026. It sits in data/raw, unchanged.
  2. Watched for a comma inside a label. One way of travel is "Combination of Rail (MRT/LRT) and/or Public Bus, with Other Modes". The comma sits inside quote marks. Google Sheets reads it right. Splitting the lines on commas by hand breaks that row.
  3. Kept only the Total columns. My question is not about boys and girls. So I dropped the Males and Females columns.
  4. Kept only Primary and Secondary. My P6 class is about to move from one to the other. I dropped the other five levels.
  5. Merged 11 ways of travel into 6 groups. Groups under 1% are hard to see and add reading load. The table below shows where each one went.
  6. Checked that the groups add up. Primary comes to 245,456 and secondary to 173,710. Both match the census totals. Then I made each group a percent of its level, to 1 decimal place.
  7. Checked against the census report. Two numbers match. 43.0% of pre-primary and primary pupils needed no transport. 36.9% of secondary pupils took a public bus only. One is 0.1 points off. I get 22.1% by car, taxi or private-hire car, and the report says 22.0%. I could not find why. It is small, so I kept going.
  8. Did not mix in the travel-time table. It counts 704,383 students, not 715,209. Its footnote says it leaves out students studying from home. It also does not split primary from secondary. So I quote the report's median travel times instead: 15 minutes for pre-primary and primary, 30 minutes for secondary.
