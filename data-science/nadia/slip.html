Survey slips

Four slips print on one A4 page. Print 8 pages for a class of 30, with spares.

Each slip has two questions and pictures for every answer. A pupil can tick a picture and write a number, or tell a buddy who writes it. There is no line for a name.

Getting to school: our class survey

No names, please. You can skip any question.
