Change notes: Changi rainfall, cleaned by Hafiz Ismail
(Hafiz is one of SkillForge's AI personas, not a real person. AI wrote these notes as an example for teachers.)

Raw files: data/raw/, downloaded from data.gov.sg on 24 October 2026.
Cleaned files: changi-monthly-clean.csv (504 rows), changi-yearly-clean.csv (42 rows).
Every step runs in changi-wettest-day.ipynb.

1. Kept the raw files unchanged in data/raw.
2. Joined the three NEA monthly files on "month". Renamed total_rainfall to total_mm,
   maximum_rainfall_in_a_day to wettest_day_mm, no_of_rainy_days to rain_days.
3. Checked for mess: 0 blanks, 0 repeated months, 0 months where the wettest day is
   bigger than the month's total, 0 months with more than 31 rain days. Nothing to fix.
4. Dropped 2026: only 9 months (January to September). A part year looks like a dry year.
5. Dropped 1982 and 1983: my yearly sums did not match SingStat's annual table
   (totals 167.9 mm and 87.1 mm lower). SingStat's footnote says its data come from
   Changi only from January 1984. From 1984 the two sources agree.
6. Left 2015 alone: my total is 1267.1 mm, SingStat's is 1266.8 mm. Wettest day matches.
   Reason unknown. Too small to change any result.
7. Built the yearly table: total_mm = sum of months, wettest_day_mm = largest monthly
   maximum, rain_days = sum, mm_per_rain_day = total_mm / rain_days (1 decimal place).
   Checks passed: 1991 to 2020 average = 2113.3 mm (MSS publishes 2113.3 mm);
   March 2025 = 550.1 mm (NEA's 2025 report says 550.1 mm).
