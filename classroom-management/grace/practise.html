Try the ladder

Five disruptions from a Sec 1 lab. For each one, pick your next move. Then compare it with mine.

These are the five role-plays from this module: a device that stays out, an argument over materials, a tech failure, broken equipment and off-task chatting. I use them with new trainers before their first lab lesson. There is often more than one good move. What matters is starting small.

The ladder, from the bottom

  1. Scan and keep teaching.
  2. Move closer.
  3. The look, or a signal.
  4. A quiet word: name, task, time.
  5. An I-statement and a choice.
  6. A talk after class.

Anything unsafe skips the ladder: "Stop. Hands up."

Try it out loud

Reading a script is easier than saying it. With a colleague, take turns as the student. Say your line in a calm, low voice. Then ask your colleague how it felt to hear it. If the answer is "told off in public", go one step lower.
