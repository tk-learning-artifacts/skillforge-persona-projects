Four posters for Lab 2

Each poster prints on one A4 page. I print them on A3 for the wall. They stay cream, even in dark mode. That's how they look on the wall.

  • Our expectations goes by the door.
  • Coming in goes beside the board, where the Do Now appears.
  • The stuck ladder goes above the laptops at eye level, in two places.
  • Lids down goes at the front, under the clock.

A poster only works after we've practised the routine. In Week 1, I point to it as I model each step. By Week 4, I point to it without speaking.

Our three expectations

Log in and start the Do Now. Lids down when asked.

Be kind

Take turns at the keyboard. Use helpful words. Try the stuck ladder first.

Look after the lab

Lab 2. Taught in Weeks 1 to 3.

Coming in

Every lesson, the same way

  1. Say hello at the door.
  2. Bag and PLD on the middle table, zipped.
  3. Sit in your seat from the plan.
  4. Log in.
  5. Start the Do Now on the board. You have 4 minutes.

Late? Come in quietly and start at step 2. We'll talk later.

Stuck? Climb the ladder

Try each step before the next one

1Read the step card again, slowly.Check each block against the picture.
2Ask your partner.Navigators: read the step out loud.
3Check the help sheet on the wall.The common fixes are there.
4Red card on your screen, then go on.Start the next step while you wait. I'll come in order.

Something unsafe, or someone hurt? Skip the ladder. Tell me now.

Lids down

When you see my hand up, or hear "Lids down"

  1. Lid halfway down. Hands off the mouse.
  2. Turn your chair to face me.
  3. Eyes up. I count down from 5 on my fingers.

"Stop. Hands up." means stop at once: both hands up, face me.
