Four posters

Each poster prints on one A4 page. I print them on cream card, because white can glare. Each one uses a picture, a few words and a number or shape. A pupil who can't read the words can still follow it.

  • Class promises: by the door, at a P5 pupil's eye level.
  • My desk cards: on the board, and a small copy on each table.
  • Stuck? Try these: on the hint wall.
  • Calm corner: inside the corner, low down, where you see it sitting on the beanbag.

Before you print: the words are big and in a plain font. They are left-aligned, with no capitals-only lines and no italics.

My desk cards

Turn one card up. No need to call out.

Green circle: I'm workingThis is where your card starts.
Orange square: I need helpTry the stuck steps first. Then start the next step while you wait.
Blue triangle: I need a breakWait for a thumbs-up. No thumbs-up after a minute? Go anyway.
