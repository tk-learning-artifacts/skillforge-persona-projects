Now and Next board

Put this on the class screen. It shows what we're doing now, what comes next, and how much time is left.

The time shows three ways: a shrinking circle, numbers, and words at the 2-minute mark. The chime is off until you turn it on. My 60-minute lesson is loaded. Change the steps below to fit yours. Your changes stay on this device.

The steps

Each step has a picture, a few words and its minutes. Keep the words short: a pupil should read them in one look.

    How I use it

    • We read Now and Next together at the start, with the class saying the words.
    • At 2 minutes, I also hold up two fingers. Pupils who aren't looking still get the warning.
    • If the plan changes, I change the board first, then tell the class. A change on the board is easier to take than a surprise.
    • Try it fast runs one minute in two seconds. Use it to try the board out, not in class.

    The board never flashes. Flashing lights can be hard for some pupils. So the warning is a steady orange box with words.
