Printables

Everything the class uses, on A4. Each bin has its own colour, its own border and its own word.

Print in colour if you can. The borders still work in black and white: solid for blue, dashed for general, double for somewhere else.

Job cards

Four jobs. They change every Monday. A pupil can say no to a job.

Bin checker (2 pupils)

  1. Stand next to the teacher.
  2. Listen for each item.
  3. Make a mark in the right row.

Weigh helper

  1. Watch the teacher hang the bag.
  2. Read the number on the scale.
  3. Write it on the chart.

Recorder

  1. Sit at your desk.
  2. Copy the tally total onto the chart.
  3. Put a dot for today.

Reminder

  1. At 1.15 pm, hold up the bin card.
  2. Say "bin time".
  3. Check the "somewhere else" tray is closed.
