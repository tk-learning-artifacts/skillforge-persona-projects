    Notes for the teacher

    • Each bin has a colour, a shape and a word. A pupil who can't tell blue from grey can still use the shape.
    • "Read it out" uses the device's own voice. It may sound different on a Chromebook and an iPad.
    • Six things is the start. Twelve is the full check. Some of my pupils stayed on six all week, and that was fine.
    • The answers follow NEA's Recycle Right guidance. Your school's bins may have a different label. Check yours first.
