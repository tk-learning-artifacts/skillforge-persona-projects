Spot the scam: three worksheets

The same skill, three ways in. Pupils choose their own sheet. Each prints on its own A4 page.

  • Circle: 3 messages. Circle Safe or Scam and tick clue pictures. Little writing. I read the messages aloud.
  • Square: 4 messages. Circle Safe or Scam, write the clues from a word bank, and say what you would do.
  • Triangle: 4 messages, then write your own made-up scam message on paper and a reply that says no.

I name them by shape, never "easy" or "hard". Pupils move between them freely. A pupil can start on circle and finish on square.

Every sender and message is made up. No real company, game or person.

Your turn

Write a made-up scam message. Use a made-up sender. No real names, games or shops. Put in at least 3 clues.

Now write a reply that says no.

Swap with a partner. Can they find your 3 clues? Your message stays on this paper. We never send it.

Answer key, for teachers

  • FunPlay Games: scam. Prize, hurry ("in 5 minutes"), link.
  • Dad: safe. No link, code, money or secret. It fits a normal day. If unsure, call Dad on the number you know.
  • Unknown number: scam. Code, secret ("don't tell anyone"), who is it?
  • Pop-up: scam. Scary and hurry ("closed TODAY"), password, a box to type it in.
  • Swim coach: safe. It tells you something and asks for nothing.

What would you do? Any answer with Stop, Check or Tell in it counts. "Ask my mum" is a Check. "Close it and tell my teacher" is a Stop and a Tell.

Triangle "your turn": look for 3 clues from the word bank, a made-up sender, and a reply that says no and names an adult.
