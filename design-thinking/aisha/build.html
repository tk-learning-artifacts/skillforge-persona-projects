Build the dock

Everything you need to make version 2 in one lesson: the parts, a cutting net, the wiring, the steps, and a storyboard of how my test went.

Measurements are ours, in millimetres. Lay your own gun on the card first and adjust. Guns come in different sizes.

Parts for one dock

  • 1 micro:bit V2 (V2 for the speaker)
  • 1 battery pack, 2 × AAA
  • 2 crocodile clip leads
  • Corrugated card, about A3
  • 1 ceramic tile, about 100 mm square
  • Kitchen foil, 2 pieces 100 × 40
  • Craft foam or sponge, 5 mm thick, 120 × 50
  • Masking tape and a glue stick
  • Steel ruler, cutting mat, craft knife
  • A pencil and the gun you will use

Safety first

  • Build with the glue guns unplugged and cold. Do not hot-glue this dock: tape and glue stick are enough.
  • Craft knife: steel ruler, cutting mat, cut away from your body, blade retracted when you put it down. I cut the thick end walls myself.
  • The tile is where the nozzle rests. The nozzle never touches card.

4. Steps

  1. Cut the net. Score and fold the walls. Tape the corners on the outside.
  2. Cut the U-shaped notch in the nozzle end so the barrel sits low.
  3. Glue foil pad B to the base. Lay the foam on it. Cut a window in the foam, about 60 × 30, in the middle.
  4. Cut a loose floor, 200 × 70. Glue foil pad A under it. Lay it on the foam, foil down.
  5. Clip pin 0 to pad A and GND to pad B. Run the leads out under the end wall.
  6. Cut a triangle prop and tape it to the front so the micro:bit leans back at about 60°. Tape the micro:bit on, with the display facing out.
  7. Set the tile in front of the notch. Flash the version 2 code and plug in the battery pack.
  8. Test with your hand first. Press the floor: you hear a short beep. Lift: a cross and two low beeps.
  9. Change COOL_MINUTES to the time your technician sets for your guns.

The full code is on the main page. Paste it into the JavaScript view of MakeCode for micro:bit.

5. Storyboard of a version 2 test

This is what I saw when student C tested version 2. Read it like a comic, left to right.

  • 1. The bell goes. C unplugs the gun at the bench.

  • 2. She lays it in, nozzle on the tile. A short beep.

  • 3. She packs her table, then comes back to look. "It's like a loading bar backwards."

  • 4. A friend reaches for it to help. C says, "Not yet, it's still got lights."

  • 5. The tick and a high beep. She puts the gun in the tray.

  • 6. The technician checks from the door. She can't read it. That's the next loop.

The test and student C are made up, like me. The storyboard shows what the code does at each moment.
