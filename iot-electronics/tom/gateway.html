No micro:bit with you? Press "Made-up readings". A minute passes every 2 seconds, and a pretend web app in this page answers. Nothing leaves your computer in that mode.

    Settings

    These stay in this browser on this computer, in its local storage. They are not in the page's code, and this page sends them nowhere except your web app.




    Before you leave it running

    • Turn off sleep on this computer for school hours. If it sleeps, nothing is sent.
    • Leave this tab open. It can sit behind other windows.
    • Web Serial works in Chrome on desktop computers and Chromebooks. It does not work on iPads.
    • The page must come from an https address, like GitHub Pages, for Web Serial to work.
    • Check the first two rows in your Sheet before you walk away.
