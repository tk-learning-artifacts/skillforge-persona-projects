Kopi Run starter For Sec 2, after two Arcade lessons

The levels, the bot and the crows are done. Students fill 3 gaps. Each gap is one idea from the rubric: an event, a variable and an if.

  • Who: my Sec 2 class of 36, in pairs. They have done Chase the Pizza and one lesson on overlap events.
  • Time: about 35 minutes of a 60-minute lesson.
  • Devices: one laptop per pair with a browser. No sign-in needed.
  • Why a starter: drawing levels takes a lesson on its own. Here they spend the time on code.

Get the starter

  1. Play the starter here first. Find the 3 gaps by playing.
  2. In MakeCode Arcade, start a New Project and click JavaScript.
  3. Paste the starter code, then click Blocks.
Open as JavaScript Open as Python

In class I share it as a link in Google Classroom. Students open it and get their own copy. I tested this code in the editor too. It runs, and it survives a trip to Blocks and back.

The task sheet

One A4 page. Print it, or open it on a second screen.

Kopi Run: fix the 3 gaps

Names: ______________________   Class: ______   Driver swaps every 10 minutes.

Play the starter for 2 minutes first. Write down anything that feels wrong.

Gap 1: no flying

Now: press A in the air and the bot jumps again, forever. Goal: jump only from the ground.

Hint 1. You need a question before the jump: "Am I standing on something?"
Hint 2. Put an if block inside "on A button pressed".
Hint 3. The question is the block "is bot hitting wall bottom".
  • Test: jump, then press A again in the air. Nothing happens.

Gap 2: kopi counts

Now: the bot walks through cups. Goal: a cup vanishes and the score goes up by 1.

Hint 1. This is an event. It runs when two kinds of sprite touch.
Hint 2. Use "on sprite of kind Player overlaps otherSprite of kind Food".
Hint 3. Inside it: "destroy otherSprite" and "change score by 1".
  • Test: grab 3 cups. The score says 3.

Gap 3: the flag

Now: the flag does nothing. Goal: on level 1 it starts level 2. On level 2 you win.

Hint 1. The variable level is 0 on the first map and 1 on the second.
Hint 2. The flag is a sprite of kind Goal. Use "on sprite of kind Player overlaps otherSprite of kind Goal". Inside, add an if-else.
Hint 3. If level = 0, call startLevel(1). Else, use "game over" set to WIN.
  • Test: reach the flag twice. You see "Level 2", then "You win".

Done early? Pick one

  • Stomp: landing on a crow scares it off and scores 3.
  • Checkpoint: a new tile halfway. Touching it changes where you come back.
  • Sound and shake: make a hit feel like a hit.
