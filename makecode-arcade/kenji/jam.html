Neighbourhood Jam A 4-lesson MakeCode Arcade game jam for my Sec 2 class

  • Class: 36 students in 12 teams of 3. Four 60-minute lessons, one a week.
  • Kit: one laptop per team, a browser, no sign-in. Projector for the showcase.
  • Theme: "My neighbourhood". The void deck, the hawker centre, the bus stop.
  • Must have: 2 tilemap levels, a score or lives, and 3 or more events.
  • Start from the Kopi Run starter or a blank project. Both are fine.

Roles

Level designer: draws the tilemaps. Coder: builds the events. Artist: sprites, tiles and sounds. The keyboard swaps every 15 minutes. Everyone tests.

Lesson by lesson

LessonWhat happens, in minutes
1. Plan5 settle. 10 play Kopi Run. 5 theme and rules. 15 plan 2 levels on an A4 card. 20 build: a sprite that moves. 5 share link to Google Classroom, pack up.
2. Build5 settle, open the link. 7 demo: tilemap, walls, a goal. 38 build, swap at 15 and 30. 5 the next team plays for 1 minute. 5 save link, pack up.
3. Test5 settle. 5 silent playtest rules. 10 round 1: swap laptops, makers only watch and write. 20 fix the top 2 notes. 10 round 2 with a new team. 5 fix one more. 5 final link. Building ends here.
4. Show5 set up a ring of laptops. 5 judging rules. 15 round A: 6 teams host, the rest play and judge. 15 round B: swap. 10 count scores. 7 awards, 2 games on the projector. 3 pack up.

Each row adds up to 60 minutes.

Sharing rules, read out in lesson 1

  • No real names, faces, school name or address in a game or its title.
  • Share links go in our Google Classroom only. Never on a public page.
  • Anyone with a link can copy your game. A link made without signing in can't be deleted.
  • Remix a game? Credit it on your start screen: "Based on Kopi Run".
  • Save a new share link at the end of every lesson. A cleared browser loses the project.

Players' Pick: each student gets 2 sticky notes for games they enjoyed. Not their own.

If things go wrong

  • Wifi drops: keep working in the open tab. MakeCode saves in the browser. Share when it is back.
  • A project is gone: open the last share link from Google Classroom.
  • A team is behind after lesson 2: they finish level 1 well. One level that works beats two that don't.
