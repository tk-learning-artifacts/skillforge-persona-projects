Duckling Round-Up starter Three missing rules, three chilli levels

The parks, the duck and the mowers are ready. Pairs pick how hot they want it.

  • Who: 24 campers aged 9 to 12, in 12 pairs. Each pair has an older and a younger camper.
  • When: day 1, 9:40 to 10:30, after they play the full game.
  • Before this: 20 minutes of Chase the Pizza, so they know sprites and overlap events.
  • Devices: one camp laptop per pair, a browser, no sign-in.

Why chilli levels? In a mixed camp, one task never fits. Everyone does Mild. Then each pair picks Medium or Spicy. Nobody feels slow, because the cards are different, not ranked by age.

Mild Ducklings count. Everyone does this one first.

Now: the duck walks over ducklings and nothing happens.

Make it: when the duck touches a duckling, add 1 to the score. Take 1 from ducklingsLeft. Make the duckling vanish.

Blocks you need: "on sprite of kind Player overlaps otherSprite of kind Food", "change score by", "change ducklingsLeft by -1", "destroy otherSprite".

  • Test: touch 2 ducklings. Score says 2.

Medium Mowers hurt

Now: mowers roll right through the duck.

Make it: a mower costs 1 life and sends the duck back to the nest.

Blocks you need: an overlap event with kind Enemy, "change life by -1", "place sprite on top of random NEST".

  • Test: walk into a mower. One heart goes, and you are back at the nest.

Spicy Open the gate. Do Mild first!

Now: you find every duckling, but the gate stays shut.

Make it: when ducklingsLeft reaches 0, call openGate.

Hint: inside your Mild event, add "if ducklingsLeft = 0 then call openGate".

  • Test: find all 4 ducklings in park 1. The gate opens and you can reach the pond.

Extra hot Finished? Pick one

  • A third park. Draw it with the tilemap editor, then load it when park 2 ends.
  • A bonus: 5 extra seconds for every duckling found.
