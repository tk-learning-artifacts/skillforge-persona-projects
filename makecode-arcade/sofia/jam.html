Lost and Found Jam A 3-morning MakeCode Arcade jam for a mixed-age holiday camp

  • Who: 24 campers aged 9 to 12, in 12 mixed-age pairs. 2 trainers.
  • When: three mornings in the year-end school holidays, 9:00 to 12:30. Snack break: 20 minutes.
  • Kit: one camp laptop per pair, a browser, no sign-in. A projector and a basket of clothes pegs.
  • Theme: "Lost and found". Must have: 2 levels, a score or a timer, and at least 3 events.
  • Roles: Driver and Navigator. Swap at every sprint. The younger camper drives first.

The three mornings

DayTimes
1. Play9:00 welcome and name game. 9:15 Chase the Pizza in pairs. 9:40 play Duckling Round-Up, then chilli cards. 10:30 snack. 10:50 theme reveal. Plan on paper: who is lost, who finds them, what is in the way? 11:15 build a player and a first tilemap. 12:10 send share link, tidy up. 12:25 one thing you made, in one sentence.
2. Build9:00 warm-up game. 9:10 demo: overlap events and the countdown. 9:20 sprint 1. 10:00 swap and play: 2 other games, 1 sticky note each. 10:15 fix one note. 10:30 snack. 10:50 draw a twist card. 10:55 sprint 2: level 2. 11:50 swap and play again. 12:10 send share link. 12:20 pack up.
3. Show9:00 polish sprint: title screen, sounds, credits. 10:00 freeze, final link. 10:10 set up the arcade, one "how to play" card per game. 10:30 snack. 10:50 trainers play every game for 2 minutes. 11:20 families arrive and the arcade opens. Pairs take turns to host. 12:00 badges. 12:20 pack up.

Each morning is 210 minutes, snack included.

Twist cards, drawn on day 2

"It's raining": add something that falls. "Bring a friend": a second sprite that follows you. "Hurry up": add a countdown. Fast pairs may draw two.

Sharing rules, read out on day 1

  • No real names, faces, school names or addresses in a game or its title.
  • Share links go into a form only the trainers can see. Nothing goes on a public page.
  • Anyone with a link can copy the game. A link made without signing in can't be deleted.
  • Nobody signs in. Most campers are under 13, and MakeCode works without an account.
  • On day 3, I ask each camper if their link may go home. A pair needs two yeses.
  • Used someone's idea? Say so on your title screen.

Judging: three checks, then badges

Trainer checkYesNearly
Plays start to endBoth levels and an ending work.One level works.

Lost and foundI can say what is lost in 10 seconds.It is in the title only.

Every pair gets one badge. Badges: Best Surprise, Smoothest Controls, Best Sound, Kindest to New Players, Cleverest Level, Funniest Sprite. Families clip a peg on their favourite game's card. The most pegs wins Families' Pick.

If things go wrong

  • A laptop fails: that pair joins another as testers, then gets a spare laptop.
  • Slow wifi: our camp laptops run Windows. I install the MakeCode offline app the day before.
  • A project is gone: open the last share link from the trainers' form.
