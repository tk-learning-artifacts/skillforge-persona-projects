Duckling Round-Up A two-park maze game, chilli-level starter cards and a 3-morning camp jam

Mama Duck has lost her ducklings in the park. Find them all before the clock runs out. Dodge the mowers. Lead everyone home to the pond.

I'm Sofia. I run holiday camps for mixed ages. This is for my year-end camp. It has 24 campers aged 9 to 12, three mornings and one big jam. I made a maze game, not a platformer, on purpose. Nobody has to master a jump before the fun starts.

Get it in MakeCode

The brief asks for a share link. I didn't publish one from this page. A link made without signing in can't be deleted later, only reported. So here is the whole game. Make your own link in five steps.

  3. Press Copy the code and paste it in.

What's inside

Events

  • Duck touches a duckling: it follows you home.
  • Duck touches a mower: lose a life, back to the nest.
  • Duck touches the pond: next park, or you win.

Variables

  • ducklingsLeft counts down. At 0 the gate opens.
  • level says which park you are in.
  • Score, lives and the countdown use Arcade's own blocks.

Tilemap

  • Two parks, 17 by 11 and 22 by 13 tiles.
  • Hedges are walls. The gate is a wall until the code switches it off.
  • Marker tiles place the ducklings, mowers and pond.

The gate is my favourite bit to teach. One block, "set wall off", changes the maze while you play. Campers gasp when the hedge opens.

Why is the pond a sprite and not a tile? I tried an "on overlap tile" event with my own tile picture. It broke after a trip to Blocks and back. A sprite does not have that problem.

Built for mixed ages

  • No jumping. Four arrows and that's it. A 9-year-old is playing in 10 seconds.
  • Ducklings follow you. You can see how well you are doing without reading a number.
  • Every park has a clock: 60 seconds, then 90. Turns stay short, so nobody hogs the laptop.
  • Park 2 is the stretch. Mowers move up and down too, and they are faster.
  • The quiet kid gets a job: navigator. They read the maze and call the turns.

Playtest notes from 2 rounds

The campers below are made up, like me. The numbers are the game's real settings in each version.

Round 1: version 1, four campers aged 9 to 12

Version 1 had no timer. A duckling just vanished when you touched it. Mowers moved at 50.

  • Note 1. Camper A, aged 9, kept asking, "How many more?" The score only said how many she had.
  • Note 2. Camper B, aged 12, wandered park 1 for 4 minutes. "It's too easy, there's no hurry."
  • Note 3. Both younger campers lost all 3 lives to mowers in the first minute.
  • Note 4. Camper C cheered louder for the duckling sound than for winning.

Changes for version 2

  • From note 1: ducklings now follow you, and the screen says how many are left.
  • From note 2: a countdown. 60 seconds for park 1, 90 for park 2.
  • From note 3: mowers slowed to 30 in park 1. Park 2 runs at 45.
  • From note 4: nothing. The sound stays.

Round 2: version 2, four other campers

  • Note 1. Ducklings got stuck behind hedges when they followed. Camper D went back for them three times.
  • Note 2. Camper E found every duckling but did not see the gate open. She stood still, puzzled.
  • Note 3. Camper F, aged 10, ran out of time with 1 duckling left. He asked to play again at once.

Changes for version 3, the game above

  • From note 1: found ducklings now ghost through walls.
  • From note 2: the duck says "Gate open!" and a rising sound plays.
  • From note 3: nothing. "Again!" is the sound of a good timer.

Starter project: pick your chilli

The camp starter has the parks, the duck and the mowers ready. Three rules are missing. Each one is a card with a chilli level.

  • Mild Ducklings do nothing. Make them count. Everyone starts here.
  • Medium Mowers do nothing. Make them cost a life.
  • Spicy The gate never opens. Open it when every duckling is found.

What I would change

The mowers go in straight lines. After three plays, older campers know the timing by heart. Then it is a memory game. A mower that turns at random would keep it fresh. It would also make park 1 harder for the 9-year-olds. So I would need an easy setting too.

The starter has a catch. The spicy card needs the mild card done first, because the gate counts the ducklings. A pair that skips mild gets stuck. I now say so on the card. A better starter would not tie them together.
