SkillForge persona projects

End-of-module projects made by SkillForge's AI personas, for learners to study, compare and argue with.

Analog Making

  • Otter Dive: a one-cam cardboard automaton for Sec 1
    A cardboard box where a snail cam lifts an otter slowly and drops it in a dive. A running simulator, a drawn build record, a true-size template, a 1-page teacher guide as a PDF and reflection prompts for a 60-minute lesson.
  • Lantern Lights: a paper circuit thank-you card for P5
    A light-up card with two LED lanterns in parallel and a fold-over switch. Build it step by step here, diagnose six dark cards in a fault clinic, and print the template, a 1-page teacher guide as a PDF and reflection prompts.

Android Kotlin

  • Hitbox: a game-words flashcard app for my Infocomm Club
    A Jetpack Compose word bank of 12 game-design words, playable here as a browser copy with a 30-second replay, plus the full Kotlin project, a quest-style starter with task card and a device plan for 8 GB lab PCs and club phones.
  • Word Wall: one glossary app, three device routes, four classes
    A searchable Jetpack Compose glossary of the 18 words a 6-week Sec 3 app course uses, runnable here as a browser copy with a replay, plus the Kotlin project, a three-level starter and lesson, and a device plan for a Mac lab, Windows laptops and Chromebooks.

Cybersecurity

  • Stop, Check, Tell: a scam-spotting lesson for P5, with three ways in
    A 60-minute online safety lesson for a P5 class with a wide range of needs: a message sorter with read-aloud and fewer-words modes, three worksheets, a rules poster with a law talk, and a reflection.
  • Six Flags Before the Bell: a mini CTF for Sec 3 Computing
    A 60-minute capture-the-flag lesson with a live six-challenge board in the browser, a law talk script, ground rules, a printable flag log, an answer key and a reflection.

Design Thinking

  • The Cool-Down Dock: a micro:bit glue gun stand
    A cardboard and micro:bit stand that shows when a glue gun has had time to cool, taken through one design thinking loop with Sec 1 makers. Both versions run in a simulator, with a 1-page case study PDF and build drawings.
  • The Two-Minute Handover: a cover-trainer prototype
    One design thinking loop with adult users: a phone screen that tells a cover trainer where a class really stopped. Both versions are live and timed, with a 1-page case study PDF and an 8-frame test film.

Game Design

  • Prime Smash: split the number before it lands
    A playable arcade game for Sec 1 prime factorisation, three playtested versions, a 3-lesson MakeCode Arcade unit and a feedback sheet.
  • Make One: a fraction card game for my P5 club
    A paper card game where you fill a cup to exactly 1 whole, playable here against a bot, with printable cards, playtest notes, a 3-session club unit and a feedback sheet.

Health Food Science

  • Breakfast Cups: food safety and My Healthy Plate for P5
    Four one-hour sessions where a P5 class runs an apple browning fair test, learns the 4Cs and makes its own breakfast cup, with a quiz you can take, job cards and before and after results.
  • Water First: sugar, price and the school canteen
    A four-lesson Sec 2 programme on sugary drinks, with a working Nutri-Grade drink checker, a sinking can demo, a student-run canteen campaign and its before and after results.

Scratch Coding

  • Blue Bin Catch: a Scratch recycling game for my P5 club
    A catch game where you sort falling rubbish into the blue recycling bin. Playable here and as a real .sb3, with a starter project and task card for P5 pairs, and a 90-minute club lesson with sharing rules.
  • Soft Landing: a Scratch Moon-lander for Sec 2
    A lander game where gravity changes speed and speed changes height, 30 times a second. Fly it here with a frame trace, or load the real .sb3. Comes with a 3-task starter, a task card and a 60-minute Sec 2 lesson with sharing rules.

Sustainability

  • Power Down Cup: a Sec 3 energy league run by a robotics CCA
    Micro:bit light sentinels and a 3 pm fan patrol measure eight empty classrooms after school, before, during and after a class-against-class league, with a live league table, a calibration simulator, the code and the full engagement plan.
  • Right Bin, Every Time: a P4 recycling project for every learner
    A six-week class recycling project with daily before-and-after counts, four 60-minute lessons, an accessible Which bin? game and printable picture signs, cards and tally sheets.
