These are the slides I showed. Most have one idea and very few words. I say the rest. Use the arrow keys, or the buttons. My speaker notes are under the slide.

    How I made them

    • One idea per slide. If a slide needed a sentence, I said the sentence instead.
    • Big type. The back row of the room was about 9 metres from the screen. I checked every slide from there before the parents came in.
    • The three ideas get a slide each, in the same layout. Parents can see the pattern.
    • The last slide lists what they can now do. It stays up during questions.
