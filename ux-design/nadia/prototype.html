What each check means

  • Text contrast: 4.5:1 for normal text, 3:1 for large text. Large means 18 pt, or 14 pt bold. That is about 24 px, or about 18.5 px bold.
  • Control contrast: tick box borders and focus rings need 3:1 against their background.
  • Labels: every input has a label. Every button has a name a screen reader can say.
  • Targets: everything you tap is at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels. I aim for 44 or more.

Sources: 1.4.3 Contrast, 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast, 3.3.2 Labels, 2.5.8 Target Size, 1.4.12 Text Spacing. The contrast maths is the WCAG formula, the same one the WebAIM Contrast Checker uses.
