✓Contrast and label checks

The script below measures the final version's colours in light and dark mode. Then it opens every screen and checks labels, button names and tap sizes.

The rules: text needs 4.5:1, or 3:1 if large (WCAG 1.4.3). Control edges need 3:1 (1.4.11). Inputs need labels (3.3.2). Tap targets need at least 24 by 24 CSS pixels (2.5.8). Free and taken slots must not differ by colour alone (1.4.1). You can check any pair yourself in the WebAIM Contrast Checker.

The safety rules in the mock-up come from these. PVC gives off hydrogen chloride when cut: Carnegie Mellon EHS. No PVC or foam in school laser cutters, trained staff with learners, extraction on: NASUWT, a UK teachers' union. Never leave a cutter running alone: UMass Lowell. PMMA is acrylic: MoDiP.
