Our team site: a guide for trainers

A CCA group of 24 Sec 1 to 3 students, in 6 teams of 4. Two sessions of 2 hours, starting in July. Students use their learning devices. Everything happens on the GitHub website.

Ages first. GitHub needs every user to be 13 or older. Most Sec 1 students turn 13 during the year. In July, those born from July to December are still 12. They take the drafter role below, and get an account after their birthday.

Before session 1

  1. Get the class list with birthdays. Mark who is still 12.
  2. Send a letter home. The site is public and account names are made up. No names or photos go on it.
  3. Ask ICT to allow github.com and your github.io address. Test on a student device.
  4. Make 6 team copies of the template. Set Pages to GitHub Actions and the 1-approval ruleset on each.
  5. Make teams with at least one student aged 13 or over in each. Add account holders as collaborators.
  6. Open one issue per student, and one for task 3.

Roles in a team

  • Account holder, 13 or over. Edits, commits, opens and merges pull requests.
  • Drafter, still 12. Writes their page on paper or in a file on their device. Reviews on the shared screen.
  • An account holder commits the drafter's page on a branch. The pull request says "drafted by our drafter", never a name.

Team rules

  1. Nobody edits main. Every change starts on a new branch.
  2. Every change goes in a pull request, with the checklist filled in.
  3. A teammate reviews and clicks every link before approving. You can't approve your own.
  4. The person who opened it merges it, then deletes the branch.
  5. No real names, emails, photos, class or school details, keys or passwords. Ever.

Session 1 (120 minutes)

MinWhat happens
0 to 5Settle. Open the team repository.
5 to 15"Who can see it?" starter.
15 to 30Account check. Tick Keep my email addresses private. Drafters meet their partner.
30 to 45Demo on screen: one change through a pull request.
45 to 85Make your page on a branch. Drafters write theirs.
85 to 95Open your pull request. Fill the checklist.
95 to 115Review a teammate. Click every link.
115 to 120Pack up. Write your next step in your issue.

Session 2 (120 minutes)

MinWhat happens
0 to 5Settle.
5 to 25Merge in turn. Fix any conflict together.
25 to 35Watch the deploy in Actions. Open the live site.
35 to 50Task 3: name the site, as one more pull request.
50 to 65Show the site. What did review catch?
65 to 115Buffer: catch up, or add a second page.
115 to 120Pack up.

If the live site breaks

Don't edit main to fix it. Open a small pull request with the fix, like #7 in our pilot. The old version is still in the history, so nothing is lost.

What I look for

  • One merged pull request per student, or per drafter.
  • One review comment that asks for a real change.
  • A live site that works on a phone, with no names on it.
