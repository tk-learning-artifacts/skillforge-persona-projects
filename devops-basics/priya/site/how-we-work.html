How we work

  1. Nobody edits main. Every change starts on a new branch.
  2. Every change goes in a pull request. Fill in the checklist.
  3. A teammate reviews and approves. You can't approve your own.
  4. The person who opened it merges it, then deletes the branch.
  5. No real names, emails, photos, keys or passwords. Ever.

When a pull request is merged, the site updates by itself in a minute or two.
