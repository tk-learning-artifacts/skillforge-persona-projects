Who can see it?

Students guess what a stranger can see in a public repository. Most guess too little.

You need: 8 cards per group: your account name, your profile picture, every file, every old version of every file, who made each change, when they made it, the email on each commit, the issues and comments.

Steps (5 minutes)

  1. Groups sort the cards into two piles: anyone can see, only my team can see.
  2. Reveal: in a public repository, anyone can see all 8.
  3. Ask: which card worries you most?
  4. Show where to tick Keep my email addresses private, under Settings and then Emails.
  5. Say: we use made-up account names in this club for the same reason.
