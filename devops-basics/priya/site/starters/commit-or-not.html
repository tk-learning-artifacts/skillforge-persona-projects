Commit or not?

Pairs sort commit messages into ones a teammate could use and ones they couldn't.

You need: 6 message cards per pair: stuff, Add drinks stall page, asdf, Fix typo in menu title, final final v2, Make the buy button green.

Steps (5 minutes)

  1. Give each pair the 6 cards, face down.
  2. Pairs sort them into two piles: helps a teammate, doesn't help. 2 minutes.
  3. Ask one pair to read out the helpful pile.
  4. Ask: what would you need to know to fix "asdf" next week?
  5. Rewrite one bad message together, on the board.
