Part A: grade your drinks (lesson 1)

Find these on the label. Sugar and saturated fat are in the Nutrition Information Panel, in the "per 100 ml" column.

Grade rules, per 100 ml. Grade each of sugar and saturated fat. The drink takes the poorer grade.

  • A: sugar 1 g or less with no sweetener, and saturated fat 0.7 g or less.
  • B: sugar over 1 g up to 5 g (or 1 g or less with a sweetener), or saturated fat over 0.7 g up to 1.2 g.
  • C: sugar over 5 g up to 10 g, or saturated fat over 1.2 g up to 2.8 g.
  • D: sugar over 10 g, or saturated fat over 2.8 g.

Sugar in pack = sugar per 100 ml × pack size ÷ 100. Source: HealthHub, Nutri-Grade.
