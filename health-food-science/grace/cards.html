Job cards and checklists

Every pupil has a job in session 4. A job gives a nervous pupil something to hold on to, and a quick pupil something to do.

Job cards

Timer keeper

Protects: control temperature

  1. Write the time the yoghurt comes out.
  2. Add 30 minutes. Write the "eat by" time.
  3. Say "10 minutes left" when it is time.
  4. Tell an adult if any cup is not eaten in time.

Cooler keeper

Protects: control temperature

  1. Read the thermometer. Is it under 5°C?
  2. Give out yoghurt only when your group is ready.
  3. Close the lid every time.
  4. Leftover yoghurt goes back in at once.

Wiper

Protects: clean

  1. Wipe the bench before food comes out.
  2. Wipe drips straight away.
  3. Use a clean cloth from the tray, never one from the floor.
  4. Wipe the bench again at the end.

Handwash checker

Protects: clean

  1. Turn the sand timer when your partner starts rubbing.
  2. Check backs and between fingers.
  3. Check they dry with a paper towel.
  4. Remind anyone who touches hair or face to wash again.

4Cs room check

An adult runs this before session 4. Pupils use it in the session 3 hazard hunt.

  • Clean: benches wiped, soap and paper towels at every sink, long hair tied back.
  • Clean: aprons on, sleeves rolled up, no food in the room before hands are washed.
  • Control temperature: cooler box has ice packs, and its thermometer reads under 5°C.
  • Control temperature: a timer keeper in every group, with an "eat by" time.
  • Cook well: nothing in today's food needs cooking. Nothing raw, like egg or meat, is in the room.
  • Counter cross-contamination: fruit boards used only for fruit. A spoon for each pupil. Serving spoons stay in the bowl.
  • Allergies: every pupil has a returned letter, and every swap is on the bench.
  • Knives: the sharp knife stays with the adult. Pupils have plastic knives only.

Based on the Singapore Food Agency's 4Cs and HealthHub's advice to rub hands with soap for 20 seconds.
