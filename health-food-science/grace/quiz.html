The pupil quiz

The same 8 questions my P5 class answered before session 1 and the morning after session 4. Try it the way they did.

In class it was on paper. I read each question aloud twice, so it tested what pupils knew, not how fast they read. Here you get the answer and the reason after each one. Pupils did not, until the post-quiz was marked.

How each question links to the outcomes

  • Questions 1 and 2: outcome 1, planning a breakfast with My Healthy Plate.
  • Question 3: outcome 2, handwashing. The checklist tests the habit itself.
  • Questions 4, 5 and 6: outcome 4, the 4Cs.
  • Questions 7 and 8: outcome 3, the fair test.

Sources: HealthHub, My Healthy Plate; SFA, the 4Cs and the danger zone; HealthHub, 20 seconds with soap; University of Wisconsin Extension, apple browning.
