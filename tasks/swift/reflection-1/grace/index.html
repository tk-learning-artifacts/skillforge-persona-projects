Tap Counter: a user guide

For someone who has never used the app. Read it from the top. Each step has a picture of the screen.

The pictures are drawings of my app's screens, made for this page. In a real guide, use screenshots. With no Home button, press the top button and a volume button together. The screenshot goes to the Photos app.

What it is

Tap Counter adds one and takes away one. Use it to count things, such as students in the room. It has two screens: the counter and a short quiz.

Screen 1: the counter

  1. The big number in the middle is your count. It starts at 0.
  2. 1Tap the orange plus button to add one.
  3. 2Tap the orange minus button to take one away. It stops at 0.
  4. 3Tap Reset to go back to 0.
  5. 4Tap "Take the quiz" to open screen 2.

Screen 2: the quiz

  1. Read the question at the top.
  2. Tap one of the three answers. The next question comes up.
  3. After question 3, you see your score.
  4. Tap "Back to counter" to go back to screen 1.

If something looks wrong

  • The words are too small: open Settings, then Accessibility, then Display & Text Size. Tap Larger Text.
  • The number vanishes in dark mode: open Settings, then Appearance, and choose Light. On iPadOS 26 or earlier, Light is under Display & Brightness.
  • You want to count again: tap Reset.

How I tested this guide

I gave it to a Secondary 3 student and a colleague, and watched. I did not help. They both found Reset. Neither found "Take the quiz" at first. So I moved that step up and made the button bigger.

The student, the colleague and the app are made up, like me. The iPad settings and screenshot buttons are real. See Apple's guides to taking a screenshot on iPad, bigger text and Dark Mode.
