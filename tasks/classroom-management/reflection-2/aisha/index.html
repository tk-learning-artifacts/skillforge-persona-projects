Three learning spaces

Where everyone stands

I drew each room from above. The blue dashes show where I walk. The green dot is my usual spot.

I teach in all three of these rooms. The rooms and classes here are made up, but the layouts are ones I'd use. Sketch your own on grid paper first. Moving real tables is slow.

1. Classroom with tablets

30 students, six tables of five.

  • My walking path
  • My spot when I teach

How I run it

  • Students sit on the outside of the horseshoe, facing in.
  • Tablets stay face down until I say "Screens up". That stops early wandering.
  • In work time I walk the outside. I see every screen over a shoulder.
  • The cart is by the door. Two monitors hand out tablets as students come in.

2. Computer lab

36 students, desks along three walls.

  • My walking path
  • My spot when I teach

How I run it

  • Students face the wall, so every screen faces into the room.
  • When I teach, they turn their chairs round to face me. Hands come off the mouse.
  • In work time I walk a small loop round the talk table. One turn and I see all 36 screens.
  • Short talks happen at the talk table, away from screens.

3. Makerspace with tools

20 students, five work tables of four.

  • My walking path
  • Tape line round the hot zone
  • My spot in work time

How I run it

  • Bags go in the cubbies by the door. Nothing on the floor near the hot zone.
  • Every tool has a shadow on the tool wall. A gap shows me what is missing at pack-up.
  • Tape on the floor marks the hot zone. Only two students go in at a time.
  • In the hot zone: safety glasses on, and a heat mat under the work. The iron always goes back in its stand.
  • A fan by the open window pulls solder fumes away. Students wash their hands after soldering.
  • My spot faces the hot zone, with the door in view. I still walk the tables between checks.

What the three have in common

Students can see me, and I can see their hands and screens. When I plan a room, I stand where I'll teach and look. If I can't see a screen or a tool, I move a table.

What I'd change: the makerspace plan only works with 20. With 30, the hot zone gets crowded, and I'd split the class into two shifts.
